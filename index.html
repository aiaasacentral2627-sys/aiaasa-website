Who we are

A home for African Adventist students studying in India.

Studying abroad means new food, new cities, new timetables — and often, distance from the church family you grew up in. AIAASA closes that gap. We're a student-led, alumni-mentored community built around three commitments.

01

Fellowship

Wherever you're studying in India, you belong to a wider circle of African Adventist students who understand exactly what this journey feels like — and who show up for each other.

02

Christian Character

Through worship, Bible study and shared discipline, AIAASA helps students hold onto — and grow — their walk with Christ while away from home and church back home.

03

Leadership

Members are equipped and given real opportunities to lead — in ministry, in organising, in mentoring the next intake — with vibrant alumni guiding the way.
